## Introduction: The Riddle of Success

Angela Duckworth begins *Grit* by asking why some people succeed while others with similar talent fall short. Her early observations lead to the central idea that sustained passion and perseverance matter at least as much as natural ability.

---

## Part I: What Grit Is and Why It Matters

The opening question leads into research across demanding environments. These examples establish the relationship between talent, effort, skill, and achievement:

- **Talent × effort = skill**
- **Skill × effort = achievement**

That framework then prepares the reader for the book's next question: how grit can be developed rather than merely observed.
